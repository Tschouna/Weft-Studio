Print guide

Every model leaves the studio already oriented for printing. Openwork pieces print with zero infill in the walls because the walls are solid lines. These settings are tuned for a 0.4 mm nozzle and matte PLA.

Relief starts at full depth on the bed, and every underside of a texture is held at 45° or less, so relief never needs supports, even 8 mm spikes, fins and blocks on a twisted piece. A 45° base chamfer gives every piece a clean shadow line. Walls default to 3 mm for sturdy prints.

Lids, sleeves and lamps

  • Lids print top-down. Glue the knob peg with a drop of CA, or press it in.
  • Sleeves print upright and nest the pot with the clearance you set.
  • Pendants print crown-down. Use LED bulbs of 10 W or less and keep 3 cm between bulb and shade.
  • Table lamps: the base prints top-down with the socket hole on the plate; the shade stands in its groove.
  • Self-watering planters: print the insert in any colour, thread a cotton wick through its floor, and fill the reservoir down the side channel.
  • Hanging planters: thread cord or macramé up to Ø 4 mm through the teardrop holes under the rim.
  • Wall planters print upright on their D shape. Hook the keyhole in the flat back over a screw with its head 4 mm out of the wall.
  • Faceted wall planters print upright too, on a small foot cut off the lowest corner: add a 5–8 mm brim so the first layers hold.
  • Lanterns are for LED tealights only, never a real flame.

Globe lamps and translucent filament

  • A round globe is closed, so it leans past 45° at the top and underneath: use tree supports, on the build plate only. Inside the ball they grow up through the opening and come out the same way. Peak top and Cone underside print without any.
  • Wall for translucent filament: 2 lines (about 0.9 mm) is brightest but shows the bulb as a bright spot; 3 to 4 lines (1.3 to 1.8 mm) glows evenly; 3 mm and more is soft but dimmer. Light colours let more through than deep ones.
  • Make the wall a whole number of lines and set Wall loops so they fill it: no infill means no zig-zag shadows when it is lit.
  • Print the glow test (Object → Download the glow test) in your filament and hold it over a phone torch to choose.
  • A closed globe keeps the heat in: LED bulbs only, 7 W or less. PETG copes with warmth better than PLA.

Wall panels

  • Every tile prints face up, flat on its back: no supports, no overhangs, whatever the pattern.
  • 0.2 mm layers, 2–3 walls, 15% infill. Print one tile and check it against the next before the rest.
  • Dovetail and jigsaw tabs press down into their neighbours on a flat table. Print the fit test first and adjust Clearance if it is tight.
  • Hidden keys are bowties that press into pockets across each seam on the back.
  • Wave ribs stand straight up, so they need no supports. For ribs over 30 mm tall, slow the outer walls a little.
  • Diffuser blocks and tall terrain print solid: allow for the weight, and keep 15% infill.
  • Terrain: type a Swiss peak or place under From swisstopo, frame the area on the map and fetch the heights. Files you download yourself (swissALTI3D GeoTIFF or XYZ, even zipped) load too, and neighbouring tiles join up.
  • Each tile's name and an up arrow are engraved in its back. The assembly map shows where every tile goes, and the wall template is the same at actual size.
  • Two-tone tips: with an AMS the colour swaps by itself; without one, add a filament change at the height in print-settings.txt.

Multicolour

  • Switch on Multicolor in the top bar. It stays on as you shuffle and open other designs.
  • Bands are cut on layer lines, so each layer is one colour and the printer swaps only at the band heights.
  • Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer: open the main 3MF; each colour arrives as a part on its own filament. PrusaSlicer has its own 3MF.
  • No AMS: print the single STL and add a filament change at the heights listed in print-settings.txt.

Listing on MakerWorld

  • Upload the 3MF: parts arrive laid out and oriented.
  • Save a 4:3 cover image in the shade you print, plus a second one with Dimensions and a size reference switched on.
  • Publish 2–3 sizes or surfaces as separate plates in one listing.
  • Paste the listing text from the Export step and adjust it.
